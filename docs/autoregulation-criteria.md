# Autoregulation criteria — Astra Synthesized Concurrent Block v5.0-syn3

The decision lens for the weekly review. Brian taps **AI Analysis** in the tracker, which
copies a structured week report to the clipboard, and pastes it into Claude Code. This
document is what that review is judged against; the block itself is
[docs/12-week-concurrent-block-v5.md](12-week-concurrent-block-v5.md), and where the two
ever disagree, **the block wins**.

Everything here is drawn from sections 14, 17, 18 and 20 of the block (the V3 source,
[docs/source/SYNTHESIZED_TRAINING_BLOCK_V3.md](source/SYNTHESIZED_TRAINING_BLOCK_V3.md)).
Nothing is invented for the app.

---

## 0. What the review may and may not do

**May, without asking:** small load changes within the printed rules; holds; one back-off
set; small rep changes; longer rest (up to 3:00 on priority lifts); removing cut-first work;
applying a printed fallback; recommending that a ride be moved, made easy or skipped.

**Requires Brian's explicit approval first:** replacing a primary lift or changing its
frequency; a volume change above 15% (overall or within a family); new deload, taper or test
changes; faster or higher-impact progression; changing the priority order; any change to the
OHP target. **Weeks 1–4: present every coaching change for approval** unless the block's own
rules already cover it.

**Never, under any circumstances:** prescribing nutrition; writing TrainerRoad ride content
(intervals, duration, power); inventing wearable-derived readiness scores or a numeric
readiness formula; advancing impact or running to compensate for a missed ride; doing any
exercise for a single set (target tests excepted); or retrospectively labelling a missed
floor compliant.

Symptom-driven stopping never waits for approval of any kind.

## 1. The conflict hierarchy

Every trade-off resolves in this order. When two things cannot both happen, the higher one
wins and the lower one is cut.

1. **Health and tissue tolerance** — symptoms, the adductor/calf/Achilles gate, anything that alters movement
2. **Strict OHP, weighted dip, neutral-grip pull-up** — the three goal lifts
3. **Prescribed TrainerRoad rides**
4. **Low-bar squat** (maintenance)
5. **Jumping and sprinting** (progressive capacity, no test)
6. **Paused bench** (and the Friday DB incline press)
7. **Conventional deadlift** (maintenance)
8. **Secondary volume**

Goals (conditional, not promised): OHP **130×2 at RPE ≤9**; dip **+50×6** and pull-up
**+45×5** at ≥2 RIR, aiming for 2; squat and deadlift maintained; plyometric capacity
progressively higher.

## 2. The load gate — the first work set tells you

Judged on the **first work set** of an exercise, against its own prescribed reserve.

| What happened | What to do |
|---|---|
| About 1 RIR harder than prescribed | OHP / dip / pull-up **−2.5 lb**; bench / squat **−5 lb**; deadlift **−10 lb** for the remaining sets |
| 2 or more RIR harder | Double that reduction, and drop the last back-off |
| Comparable successful technique and reserve, normal tissue response | Take the planned increment |
| Anything else | Hold |

No grinders, no missed-increment catch-up, one variable at a time. **No automatic load
increase occurs simply because a new week begins.** Squat increases are taken only when the
previous exposure was at ≥3 RIR. If the deadlift top double is harder than RPE 8, next week's
top double drops 10 lb. Dips and pull-ups are judged on **system load** (morning bodyweight +
added), not added load alone.

## 3. Domain review and fatigue levels

Run a short domain review — global recovery, push, pull, lower, elastic, sprinting:

- **Normal** — comparable performance, ordinary symptoms.
- **Caution** — unusual fatigue or decline, or familiar mild soreness.
- **Stop the affected work** — a tissue trigger (section 4), or performance and technique
  that cannot stay inside the prescribed reserve.

**No wearable scores and no manufactured numeric readiness formula.**

| Level | What it looks like | Response |
|---|---|---|
| **0** | Normal | Progress or hold under the printed gates |
| **1** | One poor exposure | Hold loads, rest toward 3:00, drop cut-first sets |
| **2** | Repeated comparable decline, or accumulating local fatigue | Cut that domain's work sets **25–35%**, keep familiar technique work. If only the lower days declined, do **not** deload healthy upper priorities |
| **3** | Persistent multi-domain decline or substantial symptoms | **5–7 days** at about half volume, 10–15% lighter, ≥4 RIR, no fast sprints or high-tier plyo; recommend easy or skipped rides |

One bad session is not a deload. An unscheduled deload replaces the scheduled one (weeks 6
and 12) **only** when it falls within ten days and is reconciled explicitly.

## 4. The tissue gate — outranks everything

Logged after every impact session and the next morning, for **adductors, calves and
Achilles**.

| Finding | Response |
|---|---|
| Mild familiar soreness, clearing in 24–48 h, no movement change | Hold and repeat the stage. Do not progress. |
| Symptoms increasing or persisting, reduced output, or an altered stride | Regress or remove faster running and high impact. Adjust affected lower work. |
| Sharp pain, bruising, weakness, progressive symptoms, **or movement-altering discomfort on its own** | Stop the affected impact work and seek clinical evaluation. **Do not wait for a second warning sign.** |

More than mild calf soreness on Wednesday morning (48 h after Monday's calves): hold calf load
and start Wednesday impact at the lower pogo dose. An abnormal check raises a block-level
banner in the app and holds every sprint and high-tier progression until it clears.

**Watch item:** amendment A1's Monday lying leg curl was not adopted (Brian asked to avoid
machines). Hamstrings get the Friday single-leg RDL (2×6/side) and Monday deadlifts. If the
flat sprints in weeks 8–11 bring any hamstring tightness, raise it in the review before
progressing the sprint stage.

## 5. Progression gates that are not about how it felt

These are the rails. A good week alone does not satisfy them.

- **Plyometrics.** Weekly contacts follow the approved tier table (high tier absent before
  week 5; 12 in week 5; 13 → 18 in weeks 8–11). At most **+15% a week within a tier**, one
  stress variable at a time, two tolerated exposures with normal next mornings before
  advancing. Week 5 is a named entry dose and week 7 restores no higher than the last
  tolerated dose (C04). A cut session never qualifies the next stage.
- **Sprints.** Friday only, outdoors, never a treadmill. One variable per stage — reps (week
  3), distance (week 5), terrain (week 8), effort (week 10) — and each stage twice before
  advancing (C05). Rest 2–3 min at ≤20 m.
- **Power.** Stop a set at the first visibly slower rep, a noisy catch or landing, or any
  technique breakdown — a stopped movement is logged as **omitted**. After two crisp,
  symptom-free exposures that did not slow the next ramp, change ONE thing: the next load
  step. **Never progress power in the same week as a new plyo stage or a new leg-accessory
  load.** The one-arm snatch (weeks 7–11) needs its gate: crisp swings at the current bell for
  two exposures and a snatch with no arm pull and a quiet lockout; otherwise keep swinging.
- **Arms (for size).** 3×8–15 at 1–2 RIR, last set may reach 0–1. Add reps within 8–15 at
  the next normal appearance of the **same** exercise; when all three sets reach 15, take the
  smallest load step and return toward 8–10. Hold triceps first if Sunday dips or Wednesday
  OHP ramps slow or elbows are sore.
- **Calves.** 3 sets at 3 RIR, full range, 1-s stretch pause, no bouncing. Add the smallest
  load step when every set reaches the top of the range with normal next-morning calf/Achilles
  response and normal Wednesday pogos.
- **Extra leg work (lunge, single-leg RDL).** Never cut — lighten, shorten the range or add
  support instead. 2×6/side at 3–4 RIR; load-only progression after two exposures at ≥3 RIR
  with normal adductor/hamstring response; not in a week that impact or power progresses.
- **DB incline bench (Friday).** Smallest dumbbell step after two comparable exposures at ≥3
  RIR with normal shoulder/elbow response; hold if Friday squat or dip ramps felt slow.
- **Core.** Keep each exercise for its three-week block; improve range or hold quality, then
  the smallest step — never by adding sets. Pallof is anti-rotation, not dynamic rotation.
- **Copenhagen.** Short lever throughout; the long lever is permission after three clean
  weeks and a review, not a calendar step.
- **Pull-ups (weeks 7–11).** The longer first Monday set practises five-rep readiness; nominal
  loads yield to actual prior success; week 11 may run 4–5 reps.

## 6. Time

**Strength time is a HARD 75-minute limit** in this block, a 5-minute delay reserve included.
Every session plans at 73.5 minutes or less, and at 75.0 or less even if every OHP, dip,
pull-up and bench rest runs the full 3:00. **Wednesday impact is a hard 15 minutes; Friday
impact a ~30-minute target.** Travel is not budgeted.

If a session overruns: log where the time went, then use the day's cut order below. Never
shorten a priority rest below 2:00 (squat/deadlift 2:30). If protected work repeatedly exceeds
75 minutes, **revise the program (with Brian's approval) rather than manipulating the clock.**

### Cut order by day (normal weeks)

- **Sunday** — power sets 3–4 → 3rd biceps set → 3rd bench set (press stays ≥16). Never cut:
  dip work, row sets, both core sets, cable ER, the carry.
- **Monday** — power sets 3–4 → 3rd triceps set → 3rd calf set. Never cut: pull-up, deadlift
  and OHP work, both lunge sets, Copenhagen, both Pallof sets.
- **Wednesday** — power pairs 3–4 → 3rd biceps set. Never cut: OHP and pull-up work, row
  sets, face pulls, both core sets, the carry.
- **Friday** — power sets 3–4 → 3rd triceps set → 3rd calf set → skip DB incline bench
  entirely (both sets, never one; press drops to 18, ratio stays legal). Never cut: squat,
  dip, both pull-up sets (vertical floor 8), both single-leg RDL sets, Copenhagen, prone Y,
  both landmine rotation sets.

**Deload weeks:** everything is already at its 2-set minimum; skip a whole optional exercise
(Friday incline) instead of cutting sets. **No exercise is ever done for one set.** Saturday
stays rest (except the Saturday 26 Dec makeup).

## 7. What the review must return

1. **The fatigue level** (0 / 1 / 2 / 3) with the evidence for it — which sessions, which
   numbers, which symptoms.
2. **Each prescription to change next week**, with the reversal condition stated.
3. **Anything that must be held** rather than advanced, and why the gate is not met.
4. At most five main findings; observations separated from hypotheses; missing data treated
   as unknown.
5. Plain language. Brian is not a developer and should not have to decode the report.

Ramps, power, shoulder work, core, arms and calves do **not** inflate the pressing and pulling
floors — the report marks each row with `countsTowardFloors` for exactly this reason.

## 8. Week 12 and the week-13 contingency

Tests are **split**. **Wednesday, Dec 16:** OHP 130×2 at RPE ≤9 (Monday is an easy day, every
row ≥4 RIR; Tuesday's ride is recommended easy). **Friday, Dec 18:** dip +50×6, then **10
minutes** of passive rest, then pull-up +45×5, both at ≥2 RIR. Wednesday has no dip or
pull-up work, so it counts as an easy day for Friday's tests (approved exception E5). Friday
carries **no power work and no kettlebell complex** in front of the tests (amendment A3,
exception E11). There is no broad-jump test.

Readiness singles are strict, crisp and about RPE ≤7 — they permit an attempt, not a claim of
success. One target set per lift. Stop before a grinder or an invalid rep and **do not
retry**.

**Week 13 is Saturday, 26 December 2026, for deferred tests only** (after an easy Thursday
recovery ride and no training on Friday, Dec 25). Skip any target already completed, and any
attempted and missed or ground out. There is no deadlift and it is not a training week. A
week-13 result is labelled week 13 and never becomes a week-12 achievement.

## 9. Applying an accepted change

Edit `src/program.js` — it is the single edit point for every prescription. Then:

```bash
cd "/Users/brianoliveira/Desktop/Claude Code Projects/Workout Plan" && npm run build && npm test
```

`test.mjs` enforces the block's floors, ceilings and gates (including the 75-minute limit and
the 2-set rule), so an autoregulation edit cannot silently breach them. Read the build output
— a failed build leaves the previous bundle in place.

Do **not** re-run `tools/gen-program.mjs` to apply a weekly change; it regenerates from the
source plan and would discard every accepted edit. It is only for loading a genuinely new
block.
