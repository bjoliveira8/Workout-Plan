## Time and first-two-week feedback

Normal strength sessions project to **66.5–73.5 minutes**, including full rests and delay reserve. The 75-minute suggestion is not a hard cutoff. Week-12 Friday projects to **94 minutes of strength assessment**, plus separate impact/travel time. Wednesday impact remains at most **15 minutes**, Friday at most **30**, including travel and preparation.

The fullest Wednesday impact dose leaves only 65 seconds for travel/extra preparation. Use the card's reductions when your actual travel takes longer; log the smaller actual dose. Extra Friday availability does not automatically increase workload.

For the first two weeks, record actual main-lift load/reps/reserve, power technique/quality, strength and impact/travel time, contacts/meters and next-morning tissue response, and completion of prescribed rides. Those observations determine holds or reductions. Planned loads and target outcomes have not been demonstrated.
