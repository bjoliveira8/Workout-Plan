### What would earn an A-grade assessment

1. **Priorities visible in actual execution:** OHP/dip/pull-up work and prescribed cycling survive ordinary scheduling friction; lower-priority work changes first when it costs them.
2. **Power has a progression and an outcome:** technique is established, speed remains crisp as load/dose develops, and already scheduled jump measurements provide relevant feedback. No guarantee of four inches.
3. **The plan can be repeated:** stable primary lifts and a controlled accessory menu, clear fallbacks, ordinary gym setup time, no dependence on unavailable space.
4. **Dose follows response:** the first two normal training weeks establish actual session durations, unfamiliar-exercise tolerance and power technique. They are not extra testing or a two-week replacement of normal training.
5. **A short useful log drives changes:** actual main-lift load/reps/reserve; power quality; impact dose and next-morning tissue response; prescribed ride completion; actual session/travel time. No wearable or nutrition rules.
6. **Clear adjustment rules:** isolated poor exposure means hold; repeated comparable decline or adverse local response prompts reduction of the relevant secondary work and review. Symptoms override quotas. More available time does not justify more workload by itself.
7. **Claims remain honest:** final exercise-level accounting is checked, actual recovery/outcomes remain unverified until observed, and test deferrals have a real scheduled option.

The design can be excellent on paper before outcomes are known. Confidence in its personal fit requires execution data; a grade is a coaching assessment, not a scientific score or guarantee.
