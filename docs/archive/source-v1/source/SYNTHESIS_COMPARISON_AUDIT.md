# Claude / ChatGPT comparison and independent audit

Neither source should be adopted unchanged. Claude offers a useful weekly distribution with Wednesday OHP and Sunday dips, but has important evidence overstatements, contact omissions and inconsistent exposure claims. ChatGPT offers complete synchronized cards, lower-rep pull-ups and clearer contingency rules, but its normal pressing dose is at the upper limit, its Friday is crowded, and its impact schedule cannot fit Brian’s newly supplied 15-minute allowance. The synthesis retains useful elements from both and adds no source workloads together.

The weakest consequential assumption is actual recoverability within the available time: **3–4 weekly TrainerRoad hours** does not identify intensity, and **15 extra minutes** is interpreted as each Wednesday/Friday including transit. If usable time is smaller, the impact dose shrinks. Original schedules are assessed both against the original brief and against this later constraint; the new limit was not necessarily known when the source plans were written.
