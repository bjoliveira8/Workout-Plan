# Evidence ledger — reviewed September 20, 2026

The evidence supports heavy specific practice, adequate recovery and gradual exposure. It does not establish this athlete’s optimal dip/pull-up dose, a safe universal contact increase, a six-hour recovery guarantee, or a four-inch jump gain. Those distinctions govern the synthesis.

Confidence refers to the decision’s applicability, not just the source’s design. Research was stopped once the consequential decisions had support or a documented limitation. “Verified” below means the described finding was checked in retrieved publisher/PubMed text or indexed full-text passages; it does not mean every underlying trial was independently reanalyzed. Several direct PMC pages presented access challenges; accessible publisher pages, PubMed abstracts or indexed article text were used instead. Abstract-only findings are kept at abstract-level detail. Unchecked ancillary citations in the source plans are not promoted into evidence here.
